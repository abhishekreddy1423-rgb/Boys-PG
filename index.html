About Sri Mallikarjuna Swami The Boys PG

Discipline, Hygiene, and Homely Atmosphere for Student Success

Established in 2018, Sri Mallikarjuna Swami The Boys PG was founded with a single mission: to provide MITS College students with a safe, disciplined, and comfortable home away from home. Located just a 2-minute walk from the college campus, we combine modern hostel amenities with healthy, hygienic, homemade South Indian food and uninterrupted study spaces.
